Canada's Flag

The national flag of Canada, also known as the "Maple Leaf" or "l'Unifolié" in French, features a red field with a white square at its center, bearing a stylized red maple leaf with 11 points.

The flag was inaugurated on February 15, 1965, and has since become a symbol of Canadian identity and pride.

The red and white colors of the flag are symbolic, with red representing bravery and strength, while white symbolizes peace and honesty.

The maple leaf has been associated with Canada since the 18th century and is a recognized emblem of the country.
