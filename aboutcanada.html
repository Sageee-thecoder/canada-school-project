About Canada:

  • Canada is a country in North America, it has ten provinces and three territories extending from the Atlantic Ocean to the Pacific Ocean and northward into the Arctic Ocean.
  • It's the second-largest country by total area, with the longest coastline of any country. It's border with the United States is the longest international land border.
  • With a population of over 41 million, it has widely varying population densities, with the majority residing in its urban areas and large areas being sparsely populated.
